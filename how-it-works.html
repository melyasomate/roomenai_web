N° 02How it works

From photograph to front door, in seven seconds.

Here's exactly what happens between snapping a picture of your room and your new sofa showing up on Thursday. Four chapters.

N° 05Under the hood

Three models, one tap.

Most "AI redesign" tools stop at a pretty render. RoomenAI runs three separate models in sequence — and that's why the products you see are actually buyable.

Geometry
The scene model.

A custom geometry network that infers your room's 3D structure from a single photo. Trained on 1.4M annotated interior scenes plus synthetic data we generate ourselves.

Modelgeom-v3.2
Parameters1.8 billion
Runtime2.4 s · A100
Diffusion
Restyle in place.

A controllable diffusion model that respects your room's structure exactly. Walls don't move, windows don't disappear — only the furniture, materials and palette change.

Modelrestyle-v2.4
Parameters4.7 billion
Runtime5.1 s · A100
Retrieval
The match.

A retrieval system that takes any object crop from the render and finds the visually-closest in-stock listing across our 4,200-store index. Sub-second across 38M SKUs.

Modelmatch-clip-v5
Index38 million SKUs
Runtime680 ms / object

One cart.

N° 06Questions

Common queries.

Yes — actually that's the most common starting point. The geometry model reads through clutter to find the real room. You'll get a clean restyle even if your "before" looks like a teenager's bedroom.
Tap the sofa in any render and choose "Lock this." RoomenAI will rebuild the rest of the room around it, and won't show you replacement sofa products in the shopping list.
Our matcher is tuned for silhouette, material, and scale — not exact copies of what the diffusion model invented. You'll get the closest real thing that ships — usually a within-5%-of-the-render lookalike. We show match confidence on each item.
Patios, balconies, and small backyards are supported in v1.2. Full landscape design is coming in v1.4 — we're training a separate model on outdoor scenes.
Your photos are encrypted in transit and at rest, never used for training, and deleted after 30 days unless you save them to your gallery. We don't sell anything to anyone.
Every item routes returns back to the original retailer. From our app you can start a return with one tap — we handle the paperwork and the QR code. Refunds come back to your card the way they normally would.
