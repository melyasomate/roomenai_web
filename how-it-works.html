Under the hood

Three models, one tap.

Most "AI redesign" tools stop at a pretty render. RoomAI runs three separate models in sequence — and that's why the products you see are actually buyable.

Scene model

A custom geometry network that infers your room's 3D structure from a single photo. Trained on 1.4M annotated interior scenes plus synthetic data we generate ourselves.

modelgeom-v3.2
params1.8B
runtime2.4s on A100

Restyle diffusion

A controllable diffusion model that respects your room's structure exactly. Walls don't move, windows don't disappear — only the furniture, materials, and palette change.

modelrestyle-v2.4
params4.7B
runtime5.1s on A100

Object matcher

A retrieval system that takes any object crop from the rendered image and finds the visually-closest in-stock listing across our 4,200-store index. Sub-second across 38M SKUs.

modelmatch-clip-v5
index38M SKUs
runtime680ms / object

Live commerce layer

Once you pick what you want, our commerce layer verifies stock, computes shipping to your zip, and consolidates checkout into a single transaction with split fulfillment.
