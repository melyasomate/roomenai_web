How it works

From snapshot to checkout, in one place.

01 — Capture

Photograph your room

Open the app, take one wide shot. RoomAI maps your space — walls, windows, lighting, scale — in seconds.

02 — Restyle

Pick a vibe, get options

From Japandi to Mid-Century. RoomAI returns six renders that respect your layout, light, and existing wall colors.

03 — Shop

Buy what's in the picture

Every item links to Amazon or Etsy. We check stock, shipping to your zip, and your budget — so you only see what'll actually arrive.
